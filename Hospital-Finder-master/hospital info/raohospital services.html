About Rao Nursing Home

Rao Nursing Home is driven by the vision of becoming a leader in integrated healthcare delivery space with the larger purpose of saving & enriching lives through clinical excellence. It will be our endeavor to translate this vision into reality backed by latest technology & a completely dedicated professional team. RNH provides 123 indoor beds which include 16 ICU beds, 5 Step down ICU beds 4 state of the art operation theater’s, Diagnostic services with dedicated full time consultants and a wide range of panel consultants along with excellent paramedical staff.
