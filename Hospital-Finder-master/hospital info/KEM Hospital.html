About KEM Hospital

**King Edward Memorial (KEM) Hospital and Seth Gordhandas Sunderdas Medical College** is one of India's premier medical institutions, located in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Established in 1926, it is affiliated with the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS). The institution provides comprehensive medical education and training to approximately 2,000 students across undergraduate, postgraduate, and super-specialty courses, along with programs in physical and occupational therapy, nursing, and allied specialties. The hospital, equipped with 1,800 beds, serves around 1.8 million out-patients and 85,000 in-patients annually, offering both basic and advanced medical care. With a dedicated team of approximately 390 staff physicians and 550 resident doctors, it delivers high-quality healthcare services. Funded primarily by the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, KEM Hospital plays a crucial role in providing affordable and often free medical care to underprivileged communities.
