About Sancheti Hospital

In this article, we will explore why Sancheti Hospital is renowned as The Best Orthopaedic Hospital in India. We will delve into the hospital’s advanced medical facilities, team of expert orthopaedic surgeons, range of specialized treatments, patient-centric approach, state-of-the-art infrastructure, research and innovation, rehabilitation and physiotherapy services, international patient care, and affordability and accessibility. Through a combination of exceptional medical expertise and a compassionate approach, Sancheti Hospital has transformed countless lives.Sancheti Hospital, located in Pune, Maharashtra, has a rich history spanning over five decades. It was founded by Dr. K.H. Sancheti, a visionary orthopaedic surgeon, with the aim of providing world-class orthopaedic care to patients in India. Over the years, the hospital has grown exponentially, becoming The Best Orthopaedic Hospital in India and a leading center of excellence in orthopaedic healthcare.
